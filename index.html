SIGNAL Publisher

A private publishing tool for the Unarrived YouTube channel.

SIGNAL is a desktop application that runs on its owner's own computer. It is used by the creator of the Unarrived channel to:

  • upload the channel's own finished videos to YouTube (as private uploads, published later in YouTube Studio),
  • read the channel's own video list, statistics and YouTube Analytics reports to decide what to make next,
  • publish the same videos to the creator's own Facebook Page and Instagram account.

SIGNAL is not a public service. There are no user accounts, no sign-up and no website login. It only ever accesses accounts that belong to its owner, after the owner signs in with Google and approves access.

How it uses Google sign-in

SIGNAL requests the YouTube scopes youtube.upload, youtube.readonly and yt-analytics.readonly. Tokens are stored only on the owner's computer. See the Privacy Policy for details.
