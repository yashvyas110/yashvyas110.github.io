Privacy Policy

SIGNAL Publisher · Last updated: October 20, 2026

SIGNAL Publisher ("SIGNAL") is a private desktop application used by its owner to manage the owner's own YouTube channel, Facebook Page and Instagram account. This policy explains how SIGNAL accesses, uses, stores and shares data, including Google user data.

Data SIGNAL accesses

  • YouTube (Google user data): with the owner's consent via Google OAuth, SIGNAL uses youtube.upload to upload the owner's videos and set their titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails; youtube.readonly to read the owner's channel identity, video list, processing status and public statistics; and yt-analytics.readonly to read the owner's YouTube Analytics reports.
  • Meta: with the owner's consent, the owner's Facebook Page and linked Instagram professional account, to publish and schedule the owner's videos and read their basic statistics.

How the data is used

Only to publish the owner's own videos and to show the owner statistics about their own channel. SIGNAL does not use Google user data for advertising, does not build profiles of other people, and does not use it to train AI models.

Storage

Access and refresh tokens and downloaded statistics are stored only on the owner's computer, in files outside any public location. Nothing is stored on a SIGNAL server; SIGNAL has no server.

Sharing

SIGNAL does not sell, rent or share Google user data or Meta data with anyone. Data is sent only to the official Google (YouTube) and Meta APIs, to perform the actions the owner requested.

Limited Use

SIGNAL's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Tokens stay on the owner's computer until the owner deletes them or revokes access. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google) and in Facebook settings under Business Integrations (Meta). Deleting SIGNAL's local credentials folder removes all stored tokens.
